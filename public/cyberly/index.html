Cyberly

Answer a few questions about your own habits. Cyberly turns them into a security score and a short list of things worth fixing first.

What Cyberly is: a self-assessment questionnaire and a short course. What it is not: Cyberly does not access, scan, or monitor your device. Your answers are the only input it ever has.

What you get

A score you can act on

27 questions across eight areas produce a 0–100 score, a breakdown, and a ranked list of fixes with the time each one takes.

Practice, not lectures

Nine modules with 55 activities: lessons, myth checks, sorting exercises and quizzes, in English and Turkish.

Spot the scam

Realistic phishing exercises where you decide, tap what looks wrong, and see exactly which signals you caught or missed.

Password lab

Type a password and watch it analysed on your device — patterns, entropy and an honest crack-time estimate. Nothing is stored or sent.

Privacy policy

Last updated: 31 July 2026

The short version

Cyberly collects nothing. There is no account, no server, no analytics and no advertising. Everything you enter stays on your device, and the app works with no internet connection at all.

What is stored, and where

  • Your checkup answers, score history and lesson progress are saved in the app's own storage on your device. They are never transmitted anywhere.
  • Passwords typed into the practice lab are never stored. The text exists only in the app's memory while you are on that screen and is discarded when you leave it. It is not written to storage, not logged, and not copied to the clipboard.
  • We receive nothing. The developer has no access to your data because it never leaves your phone.

Third parties

Cyberly contains no analytics SDKs, no advertising SDKs and no crash reporting. The app itself makes no network requests of any kind.

Settings contains one link to the developer's book. Tapping it hands the address to your browser, exactly as tapping a link anywhere else would — the app sends nothing, and it never reports whether you opened it.

Children

Cyberly is rated 4+ and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Settings → Reset everything removes all stored answers, history and progress from the device. Deleting the app has the same effect.

Terms of use

Last updated: 31 July 2026

What Cyberly provides

Cyberly is an educational tool. The security score is calculated only from the answers you give it and reflects self-reported habits, not a measurement of your device, your accounts or your network. It is not a security audit, a certification, or professional advice.

No warranty

The app is provided "as is", without warranty of any kind. Following its suggestions reduces common risks but cannot guarantee that an account, device or network will not be compromised.

Fictional content

All companies, names, logos, addresses and messages used in the practice exercises are fictional. Every domain shown uses the .example top-level domain, which is permanently reserved for documentation (RFC 2606) and cannot belong to anyone. Any resemblance to a real organisation is unintended.

Acceptable use

Cyberly teaches defensive practice. It contains no attack tools, payloads or exploitation instructions, and must not be presented as such.
